How it works

1. Get wXMR. Bridge native Monero to Solana at wxmr.io — minimum bridge amount 0.1 XMR. You send XMR, you receive wXMR (mint WXMRyRZh…teJp) in your Solana wallet.

2. Launch or trade. AnonPad coins are created on mogz.fun's Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve with wXMR as the quote token. Buys and sells pay in wXMR; creator fees (0.30% of every trade) are paid in wXMR.

3. Graduation. At ~112.47 XMR market cap the coin graduates to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool. Liquidity is locked forever.

4. Bridge back any time. Swap the coin → wXMR → bridge out at wxmr.io to native XMR.

⚠ wXMR bridge is run by one operator (custodial). The bridge operator holds the native XMR that backs wXMR. If the operator fails or is compromised, wXMR can lose its backing. Never bridge more than you can afford to lose.

Fees (verified on mogz, 2026-11-03)

Create: free + ~0.006 SOL network/rent. Curve trades: 0.50% total — 0.30% creator, 0.10% mogz, 0.10% Meteora. Graduation: no fee. Post-graduation pool fee: 0.50%.
